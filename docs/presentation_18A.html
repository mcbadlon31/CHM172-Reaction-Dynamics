1. Collision Density ($Z_{AB}$)

Number of collisions per volume per second.

$$Z_{AB} = \sigma \left(\frac{8k_BT}{\pi \mu}\right)^{1/2} \mathcal{N}_A \mathcal{N}_B$$

Target Size ($\sigma$)

Cross-sectional area ($\pi d^2$).

Relative Speed ($v_{rel}$)

Depends on Temperature ($T$) and Mass ($\mu$).

2. Energy Requirements

Most collisions fail.

$$f = e^{-E_a/RT}$$
Calculating...

Derivation Logic

Rate = (Frequency) × (Success Fraction)

Step 1: Sum collisions at each specific velocity $v$.

Step 2: Integrate over the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution.

Detailed Math Below

The Integral

$$k = \int_{v_{min}}^{\infty} \sigma v \cdot f(v) dv$$

Which solves to the Collision Theory Rate Constant:

$$k_{theory} = N_A \sigma \left(\frac{8k_BT}{\pi \mu}\right)^{1/2} e^{-E_a/RT}$$
This perfectly matches Arrhenius: $k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$

3. Orientation ($P$)

Real molecules aren't spheres.

$$k_{obs} = P \cdot k_{theory}$$
  • $P < 1$: Steric Hindrance
  • $P > 1$: Harpoon Mechanism

Arrhenius

$$k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$$

Microscopic Interpretation:

  • $A$: Collision Frequency ($Z$) $\times$ Steric Factor ($P$).
  • $e^{-E_a/RT}$: Fraction of successful collisions.
