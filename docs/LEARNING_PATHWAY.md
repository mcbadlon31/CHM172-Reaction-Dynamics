## Key Equations by Topic

| Topic | Central Equation | What it Tells You |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|
| Collision | k = A exp(-Ea/RT) | Temperature sensitivity |
| Diffusion | D = kT/6πηr | Viscosity control |
| TST | k = (kT/h)K‡ | Barrier crossing rate |
| MD | F = -∇V | Forces from PES |
| ET | k ∝ exp[-(ΔG°+λ)²/4λkT] | Driving force vs rate |

## Prerequisites by Notebook

| Notebook | You should already know... |
|----------|---------------------------|
| 01 | Ideal gas law, kinetic theory basics |
| 02 | Random processes, viscosity concept |
| 03 | Thermodynamics (ΔG, ΔH), potential energy |

| 05 | Redox chemistry, electrochemistry basics |
| 06 | All of the above! |

## Success Metrics

### After Each Notebook, You Should Be Able To:

**NB01**: Calculate k from molecular parameters  
**NB02**: Predict if a reaction is diffusion-limited  
**NB03**: Find a transition state on a PES  
**NB04**: Interpret trajectory outcomes  
**NB05**: Apply Marcus theory to predict rates  
**NB06**: Design and optimize a real chemical system
