2. Energy Requirements

Most collisions fail.

$$f = e^{-E_a/RT}$$

Calculating...

Derivation Logic

Rate = (Frequency) × (Success Fraction)

To find the total rate, we must integrate over all possible collision velocities, weighted by their probability.


The Result

$$k_{theory} = N_A \sigma \left(\frac{8k_BT}{\pi \mu}\right)^{1/2} e^{-E_a/RT}$$

3. Orientation ($P$)

Real molecules aren't spheres.

$$k_{obs} = P \cdot k_{theory}$$
  • $P < 1$: Steric Hindrance
  • $P > 1$: Harpoon Mechanism

Arrhenius

$$k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$$

Microscopic Interpretation:
