Single Collision Events

1. Crossed Molecular Beams

The "CERN" of Chemistry. Smashing molecules together in a vacuum.

Conditions:
$10^{-6}$ Torr (No background collisions).
  • Source: Supersonic nozzle (Cooling).
  • Selector: Velocity/State selection.
  • Detector: Mass Spectrometer (Angle dependent).

2. Scattering Dynamics

Where do the products go?

Stripping: Forward Scattering (Spectator)
Rebound: Backward Scattering (Bounce)
Complex: Symmetric (Forward/Backward)

3. Potential Energy Surfaces

  • Valley: Stable Molecules.
  • Saddle: Transition State.
  • Path: Minimum Energy Path (MEP).

4. Polanyi's Rules

Early Barrier

$$A \cdots B - C$$

Barrier in the approach channel.

  • To React: Need Translation.
  • Product: Product is Vibrating.
Example: Harpoon ($K + Br_2$)

Late Barrier

$$A - B \cdots C$$

Barrier in the exit channel.

  • To React: Need Vibration.
  • Product: Product is Translational.
Example: $H + Cl_2$

Summary

Microscopic View

We can no longer treat rate as just a number ($k$).

Dynamics Principles:

  • Angle tells us the mechanism (Stripping vs Rebound).
  • Energy Disposal tells us the barrier location (Polanyi's Rules).
  • PES Geometry dictates the outcome.
