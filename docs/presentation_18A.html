The 3 Pillars of Reaction

1. Frequency

Molecules must hit each other.

Metric: Collision Density ($Z_{AB}$)

2. Energy

Impact must break bonds.

Metric: Activation Energy ($E_a$)

3. Orientation

Atoms must align correctly.

Metric: Steric Factor ($P$)

1. Collision Density ($Z$)

How many times do molecules hit per second?

$$Z_{AB} = \underbrace{\sigma}_{\text{Target Area}} \cdot \underbrace{\left(\frac{8k_BT}{\pi \mu}\right)^{1/2}}_{\text{Relative Speed}} \cdot \underbrace{\mathcal{N}_A \mathcal{N}_B}_{\text{Concentration}}$$

$\sigma$ (Sigma): Collision Cross-section ($\pi d^2$). Like the size of a dartboard.

$\mu$ (Mu): Reduced Mass. Lighter molecules move faster.

Notice: Increasing density scales collisions as $N^2$. Increasing T scales as $\sqrt{T}$.

2. Energy Requirements

Most collisions are elastic (bouncing off).

Only the "lucky few" with $E \ge E_a$ react.

$$\text{Fraction} = e^{-E_a/RT}$$
  • $E_a = 50$ kJ/mol at 298K: Fraction $\approx 2 \times 10^{-9}$
  • 1 in 500 million collisions succeeds!

Derivation: The Logic

We combine Frequency and Energy to find the Rate.

Rate = (Total Collisions) × (Success Fraction)

But wait! The success fraction depends on speed.

We must integrate over the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution.

The Integral

We sum contributions from threshold energy $E_a$ to $\infty$:

$$k = \int_{E_a}^{\infty} \underbrace{\sigma(E)v}_{\text{Rate}(v)} \cdot \underbrace{f(v)}_{\text{Prob}} dv$$

Result:

$$k_{theory} = \underbrace{N_A \sigma \left(\frac{8k_BT}{\pi \mu}\right)^{1/2}}_{A_{factor}} \cdot e^{-E_a/RT}$$

3. Orientation ($P$)

Collision theory assumes hard spheres (no shape).

Real molecules have shapes.

$$k_{obs} = P \cdot k_{theory}$$
  • $P=1$: Perfect sphere (Atom)
  • $P \ll 1$: Complex molecule (Needs alignment)
  • $P > 1$: Harpoon Mechanism

Summary

Arrhenius Equation

$$k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$$

A-Factor involves:

  • Collision Frequency ($Z$)
  • Steric Probability ($P$)

Exponential involves:

  • Threshold Energy ($E_a$)
  • Temperature ($T$)
